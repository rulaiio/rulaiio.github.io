The rul.ai platform

A single agent runtime with two halves: a neural layer that understands language, and a symbolic layer that governs what the agent is allowed to do about it. Configured through a visual, low-code studio, so business and technical teams can adjust it without a deploy.

HOW IT WORKS

One agent brain. Two ways of thinking.

Every turn of every conversation moves through the same four stages: a neural half that understands, and a symbolic half that governs.

STEP 1

Understand

The neural language layer reads intent, sentiment and context from any channel, handling multiple intents and mid-conversation topic switches, not just a linear decision tree.

STEP 2

Reason

The agent grounds every answer in your knowledge base and systems of record, reasoning over real data rather than a static script, to minimize hallucination.

STEP 3

Govern

The proposed reply or action is checked against business rules, compliance constraints and decision tables before it ever reaches the customer.

STEP 4

Act

The approved action executes (a reply, a transaction, a ticket, or a hand-off) through 100+ pre-built connectors, with the full decision path logged.

Every response is checked against policy before it reaches a customer, and every check is logged for audit and dispute review.
The AI Knowledge Module grounds agents in your existing manuals, websites, databases and business processes, connected directly rather than exported to static files first, so a new use case can launch from what you already have. An ensemble of unsupervised, transfer, active and deep learning techniques keeps improving it from there, even from a cold start.

Hybrid search, tuned to the question

Straightforward questions are answered by fast, lightweight retrieval against your knowledge base. Harder, multi-step questions escalate to a deeper reasoning agent that checks its own draft answer against your knowledge base before it responds, rather than answering in a single pass.

Customer context

Conversation history, preferences and second- or third-party data travel with the customer, so every interaction picks up where the last one left off.

Proactive engagement

The agent doesn't just wait to be asked. It can open a conversation on its own, from a policy renewal to a delivery delay, based on the signals you configure.

EVERY CHANNEL, ONE CONTEXT

Customers switch channels. Your agent shouldn't lose the thread.

State, history and policy travel with the conversation, from an IVR call, to a chat window, to a live agent's screen, so context never resets and customers never repeat themselves.
