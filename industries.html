INDUSTRIES

Built for the industries where "the agent said so" isn't good enough.

Regulated disclosures, clinical guardrails, coverage decisions: the industries below need an agent that can explain itself, not just respond quickly.

Financial Services

Regulated disclosures

  • Handles balance checks, card lock/unlock, disputes and transfers without exposing raw account data to a script
  • Flags suspicious activity in real time and routes it before it becomes a chargeback
  • Guides account opening and product enrollment, with every rate and disclosure checked against compliance rules first
Insurance

Claims & underwriting

  • Issues instant quotes and guides claims from filing through payout
  • Gives human adjusters "whisper" guidance on complex claims instead of trying to replace them
  • Sends proactive loss-prevention alerts from connected policy and IoT data
Healthcare

Patient interactions

  • Scheduling, triage and benefits handled within clinical and privacy guardrails
  • Hands off to clinical staff when policy requires it
  • Consistent answers across portal, phone and SMS
Travel & Hospitality

Bookings & guest support

  • Changes reservations instantly instead of the multi-step hold that makes most guests give up
  • Surfaces personalized package and upsell offers from guest profile data
  • Automates password resets and account updates so front-desk staff can stay with guests
Retail & Contact Centers

High-volume support

  • Deflects the majority of routine tickets and resolves most in the first turn, with 70%+ handled without a human
  • Order, billing and returns resolved without a scripted feel
  • Hands off to a live agent with full conversation context when it can't finish the job
Manufacturing & Supply Chain

Field & partner ops

  • Auto-generates agent workflows from your existing manuals, spec sheets and technical documentation
  • Gives account managers plain-language access to order status, inventory and pipeline data across backend systems
  • Supports the full product lifecycle: discovery, purchase, usage and maintenance

Don't see your industry? The architecture is the same either way.
