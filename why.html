WHY RUL.AI

Every AI agent has picked a side. Until now.

The market offers two kinds of AI customer-service agent. Both ask a regulated enterprise to trust what it cannot inspect.

Autonomous LLM agents

  • Fluent, fast to pilot, priced per resolution
  • Behavior lives in a prompt: change it and you cannot be sure what else changed
  • The "why" behind a reply is reconstructed after the fact, if at all
  • Built for e-commerce volume, not for a compliance review

Help-desk platform add-ons

  • Already in the tools your team uses; one vendor, one bill
  • Tied to that platform's data model and channels
  • Policy and limits are whatever the vendor's settings page exposes
  • Runs in their cloud, on their terms, with their roadmap

rul.ai shows you every decision before it runs, on your data, in your cloud.
