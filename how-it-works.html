HOW IT WORKSSEVEN STEPS

From one sentence to a governed agent, with you in the loop at every step.

These are real screens from Rulai Studio, taken from the product tour on the home page. Nothing here is a mockup.

Watch the 90-second tour Read the architecture Build one on your data
Step 1

Connect your data

Point rul.ai at a database, a CSV file, or an API. It reads the schema and lists the tables it found. Nothing is written until you publish.

Step 2

Describe the agent in plain language

One or two sentences: what it should do, what it must not do. The limits you state become policies. The ones you leave out become questions rul.ai asks before it builds.

Step 3

Review the proposed flows

rul.ai names the agent, designs the conversation flows, and scores its own design against your brief. You tick the flows you want. Nothing is created until you say so.

Step 4

Actions are generated against your tables

Every flow that touches data gets a typed action: inputs, outputs, and the query behind it, built from your actual schema rather than a template.

Step 5

Every flow is a visible decision graph

Open any flow and read it: what the agent says, what it captures, which action runs, which branch fires. Edit it in place. There is no prompt to reverse-engineer.

Step 6

Test it before it goes live

Chat with the draft agent inside the studio. A damaged-item compensation request runs through the real flow against real data, and every branch it takes is logged.

Step 7

The result lands in your system of record

The compensation record is written back to the database the agent was connected to in step one. What the customer was told is what your systems show.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR A REGULATED BUYER

Three things you can hand to your risk team.

The flow graph

Every path the agent can take, readable without a developer. Print it, review it, sign it off.

The policy list

Limits, hand-off rules and disclosures, stated once and checked on every turn, not buried in a prompt.

The conversation log

For any reply, which flow, which node, which action, which policy. The audit trail is a by-product of how the agent runs.
